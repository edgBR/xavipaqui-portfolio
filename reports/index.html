Two long-term portfolios, examined

A reproducible study of two EUR retirement allocations — a 60/40 for a 58-year-old and a 25/75 for a 64-year-old — built from six index funds. It compares hand-set weights against Hierarchical Risk Parity and Nested Clustered Optimization, projects each with Monte Carlo, and tests the result out of sample.

The central finding is a negative one: outcomes are far more sensitive to the assumed return than to the choice of weighting method, so any ranking of the methods read off these projections is the assumption talking.
